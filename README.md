# NUTRIX

(formerly BEAST NUTRITION; renamed 2026-11-20. Repo, URL and storage keys keep the old name so data stays.)

Nutrition tracker built on the method of *Fat Loss Forever* (Layne Norton, Peter Baker). Runs in the browser, data in localStorage.

`src/logic/calc.ts` and `review.ts` hold the book method. Carb cycling, meal planning, fasting and the rest sit on top and never change those numbers.

Foods: USDA FoodData Central (public domain), October 2026 CSV zip. `python -I scripts/usda-extract.py <zip> <dir>` pulls the tracker fields, `node scripts/build-foods.mjs <dir>/generic.json` writes `public/foods.json` (SR Legacy, Foundation, FNDDS) and `python -I scripts/usda-branded.py <dir> public/branded/<date>` writes the branded shards; a new date also needs `BRANDED` in `src/logic/foods.ts`. Menu: `public/splendid.json`, from the nutrition declaration of Splendid Chicken. Branded products: USDA shards read on demand, the products sold in Romania from Open Food Facts as shards of the same layout (`python -I -X utf8 scripts/off-ro.py <en.openfoodfacts.org.products.csv.gz> public/branded/off-ro-<date>`, from the official CSV export, rows with `en:romania` in `countries_tags`; a new date also needs `OFF_RO` in `src/logic/foods.ts`; the products sold in Germany and not tagged for Romania, which Lidl and Kaufland bring here, with `--country germany --skip romania` into `public/branded/off-de-<date>` and `OFF_DE`; the rest of Europe that fills Romanian shelves, 1.19 million products, with `--country france,italy,belgium,netherlands,poland,austria,czech-republic,portugal,bulgaria,hungary,greece,croatia,slovakia,serbia,moldova --skip romania,germany` into `public/branded/off-eu-<date>` and `OFF_EU`), plus Open Food Facts online for the rest.
